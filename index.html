THE SIGNATURE YOU ARRIVED WITH

你出生的那一刻，星辰的位置、大地的湿度、风的走向、你的第一声啼哭，都在天地之间留下了一张唯一的签名。这张签名，叫八字。它不是锁，不是镣铐，不是判决书，它是你的出场设置，是你在这个世界上只能是你而不能是别人的那个东西，接受这件事情，比改变任何事情都更需要勇气。

In the moment you were born, the positions of the stars, the dampness of the earth, the way the wind was turning, and your first cry left a signature between heaven and earth that could belong to no one else. That signature is called BaZi — the Eight Characters. It is not a lock, not a shackle, not a verdict. It is the setting with which you came onstage: the fact that, in this world, you can only be you and not anybody else. Accepting this asks more courage than changing anything.
